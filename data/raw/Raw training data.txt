NASA POWER does not provide a complete database for download; the API works through specific queries based on location, time period, and selected parameters.
Additionally, downloading all available attributes would be inefficient and harmful to the model, since many of them are unrelated to photovoltaic generation and would introduce noise, increasing the risk of overfitting.
Scientific literature on solar forecasting shows that photovoltaic production depends primarily on irradiance,  temperature, humidity, wind.
Therefore, I selected only the 6 essential attributes, following best practices in feature selection and ensuring greater efficiency, clarity, and performance of the model.”
